# Tekken 3

**Developer:** Namco  
**Release Year:** 1998  
**Genre:** Fighting

## Overview
Tekken 3 is a 3D fighting game featuring a diverse cast of martial artists. It introduced smoother animations, new characters, and faster gameplay.

## Features
- 23 playable fighters
- Fluid combo system and sidestepping
- Arcade, versus, and Tekken Force modes
- Iconic characters: Jin, Hwoarang, King

## Legacy
A fan-favorite entry in the Tekken series and one of the best-selling PS1 games.
