# Minecraft (PS3 Edition)

**Developer:** Mojang / 4J Studios  
**Release Year:** 2014 (PS3)  
**Genre:** Sandbox / Survival

## Overview
Minecraft is a block-based sandbox game where players explore, build, and survive in procedurally generated worlds. The PS3 Edition brought the iconic experience to consoles with split-screen multiplayer and controller support.

## Features
- Creative and Survival modes
- Crafting, mining, farming, and combat
- Split-screen and online multiplayer
- Infinite replayability and user-generated worlds

## Legacy
Minecraft became a global phenomenon, influencing education, creativity, and gaming culture. Its PS3 release helped expand its reach to console players and younger audiences.
