PlayStation 2 (PS2)

The PlayStation 2, released by Sony in 2000, became the best-selling console of all time. It expanded on the success of the PS1 with DVD support, improved graphics, and a massive game library.

Key Facts

  • Launch Dates: Japan (Mar 4, 2000), North America (Oct 26, 2000), Europe (Nov 24, 2000)
  • CPU: 128-bit "Emotion Engine"
  • Media Format: DVD-ROM
  • Graphics: Custom GPU with advanced effects
  • Iconic Games:
    • Shadow of the Colossus
    • Resident Evil 4
    • Gran Turismo 4
    • God of War
  • Total Games Released: Over 3,800 titles
  • Units Sold: Over 155 million
  • Production Ended: January 4, 2013

Legacy

The PS2 dominated the sixth console generation, offering unmatched variety and backward compatibility with PS1 games. It remains a favorite among collectors and retro gamers.
