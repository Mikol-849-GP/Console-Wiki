# Crash Bandicoot

**Developer:** Naughty Dog  
**Release Year:** 1996  
**Genre:** Platformer

## Overview
Crash Bandicoot is a fast-paced platformer starring a genetically enhanced marsupial. Players dodge traps, smash crates, and collect Wumpa fruit to defeat Dr. Neo Cortex.

## Features
- Linear 3D platforming
- Tight controls and quirky humor
- Jungle, temple, and lab-themed levels
- Bonus stages and time trials

## Legacy
Became Sony’s unofficial mascot and led to multiple sequels, remasters, and spin-offs.
