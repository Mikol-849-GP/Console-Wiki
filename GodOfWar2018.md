# God of War (2018)

**Developer:** Santa Monica Studio  
**Release Year:** 2018  
**Genre:** Action / Adventure

## Overview
Kratos returns in a Norse mythology setting, guiding his son Atreus through a deeply personal journey. The game reimagines combat and storytelling for a new generation.

## Features
- Over-the-shoulder camera and axe-based combat
- Emotional father-son narrative
- Puzzle-solving and exploration
- Stunning visuals and immersive world design

## Legacy
Won multiple Game of the Year awards and redefined the franchise.
