PlayStation 3 (PS3)

The PlayStation 3, released in 2006, marked Sony’s leap into HD gaming, online services, and Blu-ray media. It introduced the Cell processor and a sleek new interface called XMB.

Key Facts

  • Launch Dates: Japan (Nov 11, 2006), North America (Nov 17, 2006), Europe (Mar 23, 2007)
  • CPU: Cell Broadband Engine
  • Media Format: Blu-ray Disc
  • Graphics: NVIDIA RSX “Reality Synthesizer”
  • Iconic Games:
    • Uncharted 2: Among Thieves
    • The Last of Us
    • LittleBigPlanet
    • Demon’s Souls
    • Minecraft
  • Total Games Released: Over 2,500 titles
  • Units Sold: Over 87 million
  • Production Ended: May 29, 2017

Legacy

The PS3 pushed technical boundaries and laid the groundwork for cinematic storytelling and online play. Despite a rocky launch, it became home to some of the most beloved exclusives of its generation.
