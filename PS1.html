PlayStation 1 (PS1)

The PlayStation 1, released by Sony in 1994, marked a major shift in home gaming. It was the first console to sell over 100 million units and helped popularize 3D gaming.

Key Facts

  • Launch Dates: Japan (Dec 3, 1994), North America (Sep 9, 1995), Europe (Sep 29, 1995)
  • CPU: 32-bit R3000 processor
  • Media Format: CD-ROM
  • Graphics: Real-time 3D polygon rendering
  • Iconic Games:
    • Final Fantasy VII
    • Metal Gear Solid
    • Crash Bandicoot
    • Tekken 3
  • Total Games Released: Over 4,000 titles
  • Units Sold: Over 100 million
  • Production Ended: March 23, 2006

Legacy

The PS1 introduced cinematic storytelling, expansive worlds, and experimental design. It laid the foundation for Sony’s dominance in the console market and spawned legendary franchises still active today.
