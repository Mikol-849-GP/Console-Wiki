# The Last of Us

**Developer:** Naughty Dog  
**Release Year:** 2013  
**Genre:** Action / Survival

## Overview
Set in a post-apocalyptic world, The Last of Us follows Joel and Ellie as they navigate danger, loss, and survival. It’s a deeply emotional journey with stealth and combat mechanics.

## Features
- Stealth-based gameplay and crafting
- Emotional narrative and character development
- Realistic AI and environments
- Award-winning soundtrack and voice acting

## Legacy
One of the most critically acclaimed games of all time, later remastered and adapted into a TV series.
