# God of War

**Developer:** Santa Monica Studio  
**Release Year:** 2005  
**Genre:** Action / Hack and Slash

## Overview
God of War follows Kratos, a Spartan warrior seeking revenge against the gods of Olympus. The game blends brutal combat, mythological storytelling, and cinematic presentation.

## Features
- Chain-blade combat and combo system
- Greek mythology-inspired bosses and puzzles
- Epic set pieces and voice acting
- Rage meter and upgradeable abilities

## Legacy
Launched a flagship franchise and redefined action games on consoles.
