# Marvel’s Spider-Man

**Developer:** Insomniac Games  
**Release Year:** 2018  
**Genre:** Action / Open World

## Overview
Swing through New York City as Peter Parker in this fast-paced superhero adventure. The game blends combat, web-slinging, and heartfelt storytelling.

## Features
- Fluid traversal and acrobatic combat
- Rich open-world with side missions
- Original story with familiar characters
- Photo mode and suit customization

## Legacy
A top-tier superhero game that led to sequels and spin-offs.
