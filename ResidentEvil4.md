# Resident Evil 4

**Developer:** Capcom  
**Release Year:** 2005  
**Genre:** Survival Horror / Action

## Overview
Resident Evil 4 follows Leon S. Kennedy as he investigates a mysterious cult in rural Europe. It reinvented the series with over-the-shoulder aiming and fast-paced combat.

## Features
- Dynamic camera and precision shooting
- Inventory management with the attache case
- Intense boss fights and quick-time events
- Blend of horror, action, and campy charm

## Legacy
One of the most influential action games ever made, ported to nearly every platform.
