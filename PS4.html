PlayStation 4 (PS4)

The PlayStation 4, released in 2013, brought powerful hardware, social features, and a refined developer-friendly architecture. It became one of the most successful consoles of all time.

Key Facts

  • Launch Dates: North America (Nov 15, 2013), Europe (Nov 29, 2013), Japan (Feb 22, 2014)
  • CPU: 8-core AMD Jaguar
  • Media Format: Blu-ray Disc / Digital
  • Graphics: AMD Radeon GPU with 1.84 TFLOPS
  • Iconic Games:
    • God of War (2018)
    • Marvel’s Spider-Man
    • Bloodborne
    • Horizon Zero Dawn
    • Minecraft
  • Total Games Released: Over 3,000 titles
  • Units Sold: Over 117 million
  • Production Ended: 2022 (most regions)

Legacy

The PS4 defined the eighth generation of consoles, delivering cinematic experiences, indie innovation, and a massive online ecosystem. It remains a favorite for developers and players alike.
