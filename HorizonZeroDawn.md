# Horizon Zero Dawn

**Developer:** Guerrilla Games  
**Release Year:** 2017  
**Genre:** Action RPG / Open World

## Overview
Play as Aloy, a hunter in a post-apocalyptic world ruled by robotic creatures. Horizon blends exploration, crafting, and tactical combat.

## Features
- Bow-based combat and machine hacking
- Expansive open world with dynamic weather
- Rich lore and side quests
- Photo mode and skill trees

## Legacy
Praised for its world-building and visuals, with a sequel released on PS5.
