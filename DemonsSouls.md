# Demon’s Souls

**Developer:** FromSoftware  
**Release Year:** 2009  
**Genre:** Action RPG

## Overview
Demon’s Souls is a brutal, atmospheric RPG that challenges players with punishing combat and cryptic lore. It laid the foundation for the Soulsborne genre.

## Features
- Stamina-based combat and parrying
- World tendency system
- Online invasions and co-op
- Minimal handholding and high difficulty

## Legacy
A cult hit that spawned the Dark Souls series and was remade for PS5.
