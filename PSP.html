PlayStation Portable (PSP)

The PlayStation Portable, released in 2004, was Sony’s first handheld console. It delivered console-quality gaming on the go, with multimedia features and sleek design.

Key Facts

  • Launch Dates: Japan (Dec 12, 2004), North America (Mar 24, 2005), Europe (Sep 1, 2005)
  • CPU: MIPS R4000-based processor
  • Media Format: UMD (Universal Media Disc)
  • Graphics: Custom GPU with 3D rendering
  • Iconic Games:
    • Daxter
    • GTA: Chinatown Wars
    • LocoRoco
    • Metal Gear Acid
  • Total Games Released: Over 1,300 titles
  • Units Sold: Over 80 million
  • Production Ended: 2014

Legacy

The PSP proved that handheld gaming could rival home consoles. It offered deep games, media playback, and sleek portability, paving the way for the PS Vita.
