# Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart

**Developer:** Insomniac Games  
**Release Year:** 2021  
**Genre:** Action / Platformer

## Overview
Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart is a dimension-hopping adventure that uses the PS5’s SSD to deliver seamless transitions and fast-paced gameplay.

## Features
- Instant rift travel and world-shifting
- DualSense haptics and adaptive triggers
- Gorgeous environments and particle effects
- Playable characters: Ratchet and Rivet

## Legacy
A technical showcase and a joyful return for the beloved duo.
