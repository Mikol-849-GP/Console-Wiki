# Bloodborne

**Developer:** FromSoftware  
**Release Year:** 2015  
**Genre:** Action RPG / Horror

## Overview
Bloodborne drops players into the gothic city of Yharnam, plagued by a mysterious illness. It’s fast, brutal, and steeped in cosmic horror.

## Features
- Aggressive combat and dodge mechanics
- Lovecraftian lore and atmosphere
- Procedural Chalice Dungeons
- Online co-op and PvP invasions

## Legacy
A fan-favorite Soulsborne title known for its speed and style.
