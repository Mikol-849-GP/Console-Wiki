# Gran Turismo 4

**Developer:** Polyphony Digital  
**Release Year:** 2004  
**Genre:** Racing Simulation

## Overview
Gran Turismo 4 delivers a deep driving simulator with hundreds of cars and tracks. It emphasizes realism, tuning, and precision driving.

## Features
- Over 700 licensed vehicles
- 50+ tracks including Nürburgring and Tsukuba
- Photo mode and B-spec driver management
- Realistic physics and car handling

## Legacy
A benchmark for racing sims and a showcase of PS2’s graphical power.
