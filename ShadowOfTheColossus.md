# Shadow of the Colossus

**Developer:** Team Ico  
**Release Year:** 2005  
**Genre:** Action-adventure

## Overview
Shadow of the Colossus is a minimalist epic where players hunt down sixteen towering colossi to revive a lost soul. The game is known for its haunting atmosphere, sparse storytelling, and breathtaking scale.

## Features
- Giant boss battles with puzzle-like mechanics
- Expansive, empty world with no filler enemies
- Emotional narrative told through gameplay
- Unique art direction and orchestral score

## Legacy
A cult classic that influenced game design philosophy and received a full remake for PS4.
