# Minecraft (PlayStation 4 Edition)

**Developer:** Mojang / 4J Studios  
**Release Year:** 2014 (PS4)  
**Genre:** Sandbox / Survival

## Overview
Minecraft is a block-based sandbox game where players explore, build, and survive in procedurally generated worlds. The PlayStation 4 Edition brought the iconic experience to HD consoles with improved performance and multiplayer support.

## Release Timeline
- **Digital release:** September 4, 2014  
- **Physical release:** October 3, 2014  
- **Bedrock upgrade:** December 9, 2019 (free update for existing owners)

## Features
- Creative and Survival modes
- Split-screen and online multiplayer
- Crafting, mining, farming, and combat
- Infinite replayability and user-generated worlds
- Cross-platform play (after Bedrock update)

## Legacy
Minecraft became a global phenomenon, influencing education, creativity, and gaming culture. Its PS4 release expanded its reach to console players and introduced millions to its endless possibilities.
