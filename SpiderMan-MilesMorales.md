# Spider-Man: Miles Morales

**Developer:** Insomniac Games  
**Release Year:** 2020  
**Genre:** Action / Open World

## Overview
Miles Morales steps into the spotlight in this standalone expansion of Marvel’s Spider-Man. It features new powers, a wintery NYC, and a heartfelt story.

## Features
- Venom powers and invisibility
- Ray tracing and 60fps performance modes
- DualSense feedback for web-swinging
- Shorter campaign with high replayability

## Legacy
A stylish, emotional follow-up that bridges generations.
