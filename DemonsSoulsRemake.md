# Demon’s Souls (Remake)

**Developer:** Bluepoint Games  
**Release Year:** 2020  
**Genre:** Action RPG

## Overview
A full remake of the 2009 classic, rebuilt from the ground up for PS5. It preserves the brutal challenge and eerie atmosphere while showcasing next-gen visuals.

## Features
- Stunning 4K graphics and fast loading
- Refined combat and animations
- Fidelity and performance modes
- Online co-op and invasions

## Legacy
A launch title that demonstrated the PS5’s power and honored a cult classic.
