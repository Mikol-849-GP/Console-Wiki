# Minecraft (PlayStation 5)

**Developer:** Mojang Studios  
**Release Year:** 2020 (via backward compatibility)  
**Genre:** Sandbox / Survival

## Overview
Minecraft runs on PlayStation 5 via backward compatibility, using the Bedrock Edition introduced on PS4. While not a native PS5 release, it benefits from faster loading, smoother performance, and full cross-platform play.

## Features
- Creative and Survival modes
- Cross-play with Xbox, Switch, PC, and mobile
- Realms, servers, and marketplace support
- Ray tracing support (pending native version)
- DualSense vibration feedback

## Legacy
Minecraft remains one of the most played games on PS5, continuing its legacy of creativity, exploration, and community-driven content. A native PS5 version is anticipated but not yet released.
