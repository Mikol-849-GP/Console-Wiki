# Metal Gear Solid

**Developer:** Konami  
**Release Year:** 1998  
**Genre:** Stealth Action

## Overview
Play as Solid Snake, infiltrating Shadow Moses to stop a nuclear threat. Metal Gear Solid introduced cinematic gameplay, voice acting, and stealth mechanics to the mainstream.

## Features
- Tactical espionage action
- Codec-based story delivery
- Unique boss fights (e.g. Psycho Mantis)
- Deep themes: war, identity, and control

## Legacy
Spawned a legendary franchise and set the standard for narrative-driven action games.
