# LittleBigPlanet

**Developer:** Media Molecule  
**Release Year:** 2008  
**Genre:** Platformer / Creative Sandbox

## Overview
LittleBigPlanet is a whimsical platformer starring Sackboy, where players can create, share, and play custom levels. It emphasizes creativity and community.

## Features
- Drag-and-drop level editor
- Cooperative and competitive multiplayer
- Physics-based puzzles
- Thousands of user-generated levels

## Legacy
Pioneered user-generated content on consoles and inspired a creative movement.
