PlayStation 5 (PS5)

The PlayStation 5, released in 2020, introduced lightning-fast SSD storage, ray tracing, and immersive haptics via the DualSense controller. It’s built for speed, fidelity, and next-gen experiences.

Key Facts

  • Launch Dates: North America, Japan, Australia (Nov 12, 2020); Europe (Nov 19, 2020)
  • CPU: 8-core AMD Zen 2
  • Media Format: Ultra HD Blu-ray / Digital
  • Graphics: AMD RDNA 2 GPU with ray tracing
  • Iconic Games:
    • Demon’s Souls (Remake)
    • Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart
    • Spider-Man: Miles Morales
    • Horizon Forbidden West
    • Minecraft
  • Total Games Released: 500+ titles (and growing)
  • Units Sold: Over 50 million (as of 2025)

Legacy

The PS5 continues Sony’s legacy of powerful hardware and exclusive titles, pushing boundaries in speed, immersion, and visual fidelity. It’s a console built for the future.
