# Final Fantasy VII

**Developer:** Square  
**Release Year:** 1997  
**Genre:** Role-playing (JRPG)

## Overview
Final Fantasy VII follows Cloud Strife and his allies as they battle the corrupt Shinra Corporation and the mysterious Sephiroth. It redefined RPGs with cinematic storytelling and 3D graphics.

## Features
- Materia-based magic and customization
- Turn-based combat with Limit Breaks
- Expansive world map and mini-games
- Iconic soundtrack by Nobuo Uematsu

## Legacy
One of the most influential RPGs ever made. It inspired sequels, spin-offs, and a full remake trilogy.
